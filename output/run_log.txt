Why preprocessing helps:
- Lowercasing stops 'Good' and 'good' being treated as different features.
- Removing punctuation and extra whitespace reduces noise in the vocabulary.
- A consistent form makes counts, matching and models behave more predictably.
- Some tasks need the opposite (e.g. sentiment with '!!!' or named entities in case),
  so each step is optional and chosen per task.
